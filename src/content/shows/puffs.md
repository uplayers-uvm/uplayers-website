*Puffs, or: Seven Increasingly Eventful Years at a Certain School of Magic and Magic* is a hilarious, heartfelt comedy that follows the "other" students at a certain famous wizarding school: the lovable underdogs who were never the heroes of the story.

UPlayers' fall 2025 main stage production featured a cast of eleven, with seven actors playing more than 40 roles between them!
