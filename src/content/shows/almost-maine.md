*Almost, Maine* is a collection of 11 vignettes that show the struggles of everyday love and life. The people of Almost, an unorganized territory tucked away in Northern Maine, show the unmagical 'magic' of everyday, small-town interactions.

From crushing a few Buds at the Moose Paddy to learning what to fear, *Almost, Maine* takes the audience through a series of regular scenarios, containing the thoroughly unscientific motions of love, all of which are reflected with the very scientific light show that is the aurora borealis.

## Meet the directors

**Anna Blackburn** is a Special Education major with a minor in Spanish & Theatre. She did *Puffs* and *ABH* with UPlayers and many other shows, including *The 25th Annual Putnam County Spelling Bee*, *Hadestown*, *Ordinary Days*, *The Prom*, and *School of Rock*.

**Zeke Lorenzo** is a sophomore Social Work major. They started doing theatre in high school, designing sets for *Argonautica* and *Hadestown*. In their free time, they love to read, hike and play music. They are super excited to be co-directing *Almost, Maine* and cannot wait to get started!
