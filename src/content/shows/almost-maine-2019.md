UPlayers' spring 2019 production followed the residents of the town of Almost, Maine as they fall in and out of love over one midwinter night, with a few supernatural twists along the way. Many of the cast were new to UVM or to theater altogether. (UPlayers brought the show back in Fall 2026!)
