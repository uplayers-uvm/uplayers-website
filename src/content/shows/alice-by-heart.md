Join University Players "Down The Hole" in *Alice By Heart*, the spring musical!

Alice Spencer seeks comfort in the pages of Lewis Carroll's *Alice's Adventures in Wonderland*. As they travel through the classic novel, running into caterpillars and cats and lobsters and more, Alice and Alfred are forced to confront age-old questions about grief, loss, and love.
