## Join us on the Lynx!

In order to ensure we get appropriate funding each year, we have to provide proof that you are a member of our club! It doesn't take long to join, we promise!

1. Follow [this link to the Lynx](https://thelynx.campuslabs.com/engage/organizations?categories=3435)
2. Log in
3. Select **The University Players** from the clubs list
4. Click the blue **Join** button

A little work from you will make all the difference to us!

## Get involved

UPlayers puts on two main stage productions each season: one in the fall, one in the spring, with a musical every alternate spring. Actors, directors, writers, designers, techies and stage managers are all welcome. No experience needed.
