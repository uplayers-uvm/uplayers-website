## Join us on Rallypoint!

In order to ensure we get appropriate funding each year, we have to provide proof that you are a member of our club! It doesn't take long to join, we promise!

1. Follow [This link to Rallypoint](https://rallypoint.uvm.edu/)
2. Log in
3. Select **The University Players** from the clubs list
4. Click the **Join** button

A little work from you will make all the difference to us!

## Get involved

UPlayers puts on two main stage productions each season: one in the fall, one in the spring, with a musical every alternate spring. Every spring we also do Fringe Festival: a series of short, student written and directed plays performed in Ira Allen Chapel. Actors, directors, writers, designers, techies and stage managers are all welcome. No experience needed.
