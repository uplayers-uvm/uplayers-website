## Do I need acting experience?
No! UPlayers is open to anyone with an interest in theatre: acting, directing, writing, design, tech or stage management.

## How do I join?
Join us on [the Lynx](/join) and email [uplayers@uvm.edu](mailto:uplayers@uvm.edu) to get on our mailing list.

## How many shows do you do?
Two main stage productions each season, one in the fall and one in the spring, with a musical every other spring.
