The University Players (UPlayers) is UVM's student-run theatre club. Everything we do, from writing and directing to design, tech and acting, is done by students, and you never need to be a theatre major or minor to join.

> "Everyone has a place in theater." — Daniel Kelley, UPlayers member (2021)

## What we do

- **Two main stage productions a season**, one in the fall and one in the spring, with a musical every other spring.
- **Fringe Fest:** an annual festival of short one-act plays written, directed and performed by students, inspired by the Edinburgh Festival Fringe.
- **Original student work:** members have written and staged their own full-length plays.
- **Socials and open mics:** cabarets, movie nights and more throughout the year.

## A timeline

**2015:** Senior Sarah Evans writes and directs the original comedy [*What Happened After Superhero Con*](/shows/what-happened-after-superhero-con), UPlayers' first completely student-produced play of the year.

**2017:** Evans returns with [*Like Any Other Woman*](/shows/like-any-other-woman), her honors-thesis play about Susanna Salter, the first woman elected mayor in the U.S., staged at Main Street Landing.

**2018:** The spring musical [*The 25th Annual Putnam County Spelling Bee*](/shows/putnam-county-spelling-bee) brings audience volunteers on stage to spell.

**2019:** [*Almost, Maine*](/shows/almost-maine-2019) in the spring and [*Rosencrantz and Guildenstern Are Dead*](/shows/rosencrantz-and-guildenstern-are-dead) in the fall. The club's improv side becomes its own group, and the first Fringe Fest is held.

**2020–21:** During the pandemic, members kept creating online, including **Red Eye**, a festival where students had just 24 hours to write and perform a play or skit.

**2021:** Back on stage with [*Arsenic and Old Lace*](/shows/arsenic-and-old-lace).

**2022:** The fourth annual [Fringe Fest](/events/fringe-fest-2022) fills Ira Allen Chapel, followed by [*Love/Sick*](/shows/love-sick) in the spring and [*Mircalla*](/shows/mircalla) in the fall.

**2023:** [*Waiting for Godot*](/shows/waiting-for-godot) and [*The Curious Savage*](/shows/the-curious-savage).

**2025:** [*Clue*](/shows/clue).

**2026:** The spring musical [*Alice By Heart*](/shows/alice-by-heart) at Isham Family Farm, then [*Almost, Maine*](/shows/almost-maine) returns this fall.
