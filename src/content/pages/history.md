The University Players (UPlayers) is UVM's student-run theatre club. Everything we do, from writing and directing to design, tech and acting, is done by students, and you never need to be a theatre major or minor to join.

> "Everyone has a place in theater." — Daniel Kelley, UPlayers member (2021)

## What we do

- **Two main stage productions a season**, one in the fall and one in the spring, with a musical every other spring.
- **Fringe Fest:** our annual festival of one-act plays written, directed and performed entirely by students, inspired by the Edinburgh Festival Fringe. 2026 marked its eighth year.
- **Original student work:** members regularly write and stage their own plays, from Fringe one-acts to full main stage productions like *Renew* (2024).
- **Workshops:** acting, directing, playwriting, improv, tech and even graphic design, all open to every skill level.
- **Socials:** cabarets and open mics at Brennan's, our yearly Rocky Horror night, watch parties and more.

## A timeline

**2015:** Senior Sarah Evans writes and directs the original comedy [*What Happened After Superhero Con*](/shows/what-happened-after-superhero-con), UPlayers' first completely student-produced play of the year.

**2017:** Evans returns with [*Like Any Other Woman*](/shows/like-any-other-woman), her honors-thesis play about Susanna Salter, the first woman elected mayor in the U.S.

**2018:** The spring musical [*The 25th Annual Putnam County Spelling Bee*](/shows/putnam-county-spelling-bee) brings audience volunteers on stage to spell.

**2019:** [*Almost, Maine*](/shows/almost-maine-2019) in the spring and [*Rosencrantz and Guildenstern Are Dead*](/shows/rosencrantz-and-guildenstern-are-dead) in the fall. The club's improv side becomes its own group, and the very first Fringe Fest takes place.

**2020–21:** The pandemic can't stop us. Members keep creating through **Red Eye**, a festival where students write, direct and act in a ten-minute play put together in just 24 hours, and a socially distanced, livestreamed spring Fringe Festival.

**2021:** Back on stage at Main Street Landing's Black Box Theater with [*Arsenic and Old Lace*](/shows/arsenic-and-old-lace).

**2022:** [*Love/Sick*](/shows/love-sick) in the spring, the fourth annual [Fringe Fest](/events/fringe-fest-2022) at Ira Allen Chapel, and the gothic [*Mircalla*](/shows/mircalla) in the fall.

**2023:** Beckett's [*Waiting for Godot*](/shows/waiting-for-godot), [Fringe Fest 2023](/events/fringe-fest-2023), ten-minute plays, and [*The Curious Savage*](/shows/the-curious-savage).

**2024:** The spring musical [*You're A Good Man, Charlie Brown*](/shows/youre-a-good-man-charlie-brown), [Fringe Fest 2024](/events/fringe-fest-2024), and [*Renew*](/shows/renew), an original Thanksgiving family drama written and directed by student Trent Snyder.

**2025:** The seventh annual [Fringe Fest](/events/fringe-fest-2025), the comedy-whodunit [*Clue: On Stage*](/shows/clue), and [*Puffs*](/shows/puffs), with seven actors playing over 40 roles.

**2026:** [Fringe Fest 2026](/events/fringe-fest-2026), the spring musical [*Alice By Heart*](/shows/alice-by-heart) in a barn at Isham Family Farm, and [*Almost, Maine*](/shows/almost-maine) returns this fall.
