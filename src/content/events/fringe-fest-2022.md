Inspired by the Edinburgh Festival Fringe, UPlayers' fourth annual Fringe Fest featured a series of short one-act plays written, directed, produced and performed entirely by students, including Evan Dodge's *The Origin of Annoyance*. Casts rehearsed from January, and it was our first unmasked festival since the COVID-19 pandemic began.

> "No matter what position you are in — an actor, director or crew member — everyone is very supportive of each other." — Emma Brazinski, director
